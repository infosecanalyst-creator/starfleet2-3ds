# What this game is
@shelf START
@see Training 2.0 Your Missions
HOW: 1) You command one Krellan battlecruiser. 2) Each mission gives you written orders for one region of space: tap ORDERS to read them again at any time. 3) You fly out from a starfort, explore planets, raid or fight the Alliance (UGA), and come back to a starfort.

WHY: This is a simulation, not an arcade game. The ship does what you order, in real time, and your officers report back in words. There are 35 missions in seven levels, from Altern up to Admiral.

# Your first flight (Tutorial 1)
@shelf START
@see Training 3.5 Test Flights
@see Tutorial 1: Basic Navigation
HOW: 1) At sign-on tap Tutorials, then 1. 2) Tap CONTINUE at the motto: you are on the bridge, docked at a starfort. 3) A checklist starts by itself on the top screen and ticks each step as you do it. 4) The first steps are: SYSTEM, tap the planet, TARGETER, ENTER, B, then LAUNCH. 5) It comes in three short parts: 1A fly to a planet and orbit, 1B probe and jump to a star, 1C come home. 6) Press L to freeze whenever you want to think. Stuck at any point? Press SELECT: it says what to do next.

WHY: A tutorial does not count on your service record, so nothing you break matters. Tutorial 1 starts on the bridge with no orders to read first, and it teaches the three things every mission needs: the maps, the Targeter and the engines.

NEXT: When Tutorial 1 is done, fly Practice mission 1-A. The card "Your first mission (1-A)" walks you through it.

IF IT DOESN'T WORK: ORDERS and Save are switched off inside a tutorial: that is normal. If the ship stays where it is, read the card "Ship won't move". If you are lost in menus, press B until you are back on the bridge.

# Freeze: stop the clock while you think
@shelf START
@see Training 2.4 Ship and Simulation Controls
HOW: 1) Press L or tap FREEZE. 2) Nothing on the game's own screens changes: only the clock stops (watch MsnElpTme on the top screen). 3) Look at any display, read your orders, change settings. 4) Press L again to carry on.

WHY: Everything happens in real time, so stopping the clock is the strongest help you have. Enemies cannot move or shoot while you read.

IF IT DOESN'T WORK: While frozen you cannot give orders that take time. A few, such as activating the teleporters, start the clock again by themselves.

# Saving your game
@shelf START
@see Ops 1.4 Computer Control
HOW: 1) The game autosaves by itself during a mission. 2) For a save with a name: tap SHIP MENUS, then COMPUTER, then Special services, then Save, and type a name of up to 8 characters. 3) The port's quick save then saves again under that name. 4) To stop playing: SHIP MENUS, COMPUTER, Special services, Quit. Answer YES to "Do you want to stop?", then NO to "Do you wish to give up on this mission?" (YES there throws the mission away).

WHY: Resume at sign-on continues from your last named save or autosave. The second question after Quit is the dangerous one: giving up a mission cannot be undone.

IF IT DOESN'T WORK: Save does nothing inside a tutorial. Save while the bridge is quiet. If messages are still arriving, wait for them to finish and try again.

# Time: days left and why to hurry
@shelf START
@see Training 2.2 Bridge Display
@see Ops 1.1 Navigation Control
HOW: 1) On the top screen find MsnElpTme (days used) and Time left (days remaining). 2) Before a trip, the Targeter box shows Time (days the trip takes) beside Time left: compare them. 3) Cross between stars at hyperspace speed 8. 4) When the LOW TME light turns red, less than 10% of your time remains: finish up and head for a starfort.

WHY: Your orders must be completed in the time allotted. The clock runs while the ship moves or orbits. It stood still while docked and while stopped in open space, and it stops when you freeze the game.

# How a mission flows
@shelf START
@see Ops 4.1 Conquering a Region
@see Tutorial 1: Basic Navigation
HOW: 1) Read ORDERS. 2) LAUNCH from the starfort. 3) Tap STARS, send sensor probes to find star systems with planets, pick one and travel there in hyperspace. 4) In each system visit the planets: orbit, scan, send landing parties; at higher ranks fight ships and invade. 5) Tap ORDERS to see which objectives say COMPLETED. 6) Tap STARFORT and dock.

WHY: Objectives are met out in the region, but repairs, supplies and fresh crew come from starforts. The Training Manual says you are required to return to a starfort at the end of each mission.

# Reading the top screen
@shelf BRIDGE
@see Training 2.2 Bridge Display
HOW: 1) Top row: eight alert lights; one that stays red is a problem. 2) LRS: the wide radar around your ship. 3) COMMS: what your officers just told you. 4) DAMAGE, DATA and ENERGY: the ship's health, stores and power. 5) STATUS: GREEN, ORANGE, RED or DOCKED. 6) The other cards on this BRIDGE shelf of the manual (START > MANUAL) explain each panel.

WHY: You watch the top screen and act on the touch screen. When an order seems to do nothing, the reason is almost always a red light or the newest COMMS line.

# Alert lights (top row)
@shelf BRIDGE
@see Training 2.2 Bridge Display
HOW: 1) A light that stays red is a live problem. 2) LOW PWR: under 25% energy. LOW SUP: under 25% supplies. LOW TME: under 10% of mission time. MEDICAL: crew exhausted, sick or low in morale. SEC ALT: security alert inside your ship. MINES: enemy mines nearby. DISTRSS: other Krellan forces call for help. HULL PN: over half the hull is destroyed.

WHY: The computer checks the eight lights in turn, so each flickers briefly as it is tested. Only a light that stays lit matters. An officer also reports the problem in COMMS and an alarm sounds.

IF IT DOESN'T WORK: LOW PWR, LOW SUP and HULL PN are all cured by docking: tap STARFORT. For MEDICAL see the card "Crew tired or morale low".

# Long Range Scan (LRS)
@shelf BRIDGE
@see Training 2.2 Bridge Display
HOW: 1) Your ship is the centre; the top of the grid is where your nose points. 2) It shows 20 points in every direction. 3) Green dot: Krellan ship. Red dot: Alliance ship. Cyan block: planet. Green block: starfort. Red block: starbase. Yellow block: the star. 4) To close in on something, turn until it sits on the line straight above the centre.

WHY: The tactical display only sees 5 points around you. The LRS shows what is coming before it gets there, without symbols, only coloured dots.

IF IT DOESN'T WORK: A black and white pattern instead of dots means the long range sensors are not working (LRS in the DAMAGE panel).

# Tactical display
@shelf BRIDGE
@see Training 2.2 Bridge Display
@see Training 2.3 Display Symbols
HOW: 1) Your ship is always in the centre, nose up. 2) Tap anywhere on it to steer toward that point. 3) The red numbers on the edges count distance in points, 5 each way. 4) Small red dots are torpedoes or mines: keep clear of them. 5) Hold R and tap a symbol to have it explained.

WHY: This is the close-up view where fighting, docking and towing happen. Phasers only reach targets you can see here.

IF IT DOESN'T WORK: Coloured streaks instead of a grid mean you are in hyperspace. A planet in the middle with rings of dots means you are in orbit: see "Orbital display".

# Navigation data (NAV)
@shelf BRIDGE
@see Training 2.2 Bridge Display
HOW: 1) Reg. Loc: where you are in the region (light years). 2) Position: where you are in this system (points), or your orbit and place in it. 3) Course: the direction you have set. 4) Target and Dist Trg: where the navigation computer is taking you and how far it is. 5) Mode: what the ship is doing now.

WHY: Course is what you asked for; the heading number above the tactical display is where the nose points now. The ship swings round until the two match.

IF IT DOESN'T WORK: Modes: DOCKED, NORM SPC (normal space), HYPRSPCE (hyperspace), EMG STOP (stopping), PURSUIT (chasing a ship), IN TOW (being towed), SYS SRCH (searching the system), PATROL (circling a point).

# Velocity (VEL)
@shelf BRIDGE
@see Training 2.2 Bridge Display
HOW: 1) Two bars: H is hyperspace speed, 0 to 10; S is sublight speed, 0.0 to 0.9. 2) The small arrows mark the speed you have set. 3) Below, s: is the speed set and c: is the speed now. 4) SPD+ and SPD- or Circle Pad up and down change sublight; ZR and ZL change hyperspace speed.

WHY: Setting a speed does not start the ship. The bars only climb after the engines are started: s: 0.9 with c: 0.0 means "ready, but engines off".

IF IT DOESN'T WORK: You cannot change speed while flying in hyperspace, only stop. Top sublight speed is 0.9; safe hyperspace speed is 8.

# Messages (COMMS) and the instruction line
@shelf BRIDGE
@see Training 2.2 Bridge Display
@see Ops 1.6 Communications Control
HOW: 1) New reports appear at the bottom of COMMS and push older lines up. 2) The line under it shows what the game wants now, such as "Select option" or a yes/no question. 3) To reread old lines tap LOG, or SHIP MENUS, COMMS in the menu row for the logs.

WHY: Your officers answer every order in words. "Engines engaged" means it worked; anything else tells you what is missing. Reading the last line fixes most problems.

# Systems status (DAMAGE)
@shelf BRIDGE
@see Training 2.2 Bridge Display
@see Ops 1.2 Engineering Control
HOW: 1) Each name is a ship system; its background colour is its health. 2) Green: working. Yellow: damaged, partly working. Red: not working. Black: destroyed. 3) To speed up repairs tap SHIP MENUS, then ENGINEERING, then Damage control, give repair teams to a system with + and -, and press A.

WHY: Damaged systems repair themselves slowly, faster with teams assigned. A destroyed (black) system cannot be fixed in space: only a starfort can replace it.

# DATA: ship's stores
@shelf BRIDGE
@see Training 2.2 Bridge Display
HOW: 1) Torps: torpedoes left. Crew and Shk Troops: people aboard. Supplies: percent left. Oblitr Pods: planet-killer bombs. Escorts: destroyers assigned to you. 2) Snsr Probes shows L: probes loaded in the launchers and S: probes in storage. 3) In dock, a number in brackets after Escorts counts escorts docked with you; green with an up arrow means they are ready.

WHY: These are the things you run out of. Supplies and energy fall all the time; torpedoes, probes and crew fall when you use or lose them. A starfort refills them all.

# ENERGY
@shelf BRIDGE
@see Training 2.2 Bridge Display
@see Ops 1.2 Engineering Control
HOW: 1) Usage: how fast you are spending power now. 2) Quantity: energy left; under 25% lights LOW PWR. 3) Backup: the small emergency reserve. 4) Shields: shield strength; SHLD+ and SHLD- change it by 5%. 5) For exact numbers tap SHIP MENUS, then ENGINEERING, then Power, then Status.

WHY: Engines, shields, the tractor beam and teleporters all drink energy, and every hit your shields stop is paid for in energy too. Shields below 100% let part of a hit through to the hull.

IF IT DOESN'T WORK: If Quantity is empty and only Backup is left: lower shields, stop, and get energy teleported from another Krellan ship, or ask for a tow.

# STATUS: your alert condition
@shelf BRIDGE
@see Training 2.2 Bridge Display
HOW: 1) DOCKED: at a starfort or captured starbase. 2) GREEN: no enemy about; the crew rests. 3) ORANGE: in Alliance space, nothing in range; shields and weapons powered down, crew on edge. 4) RED: enemy ship or starbase detected, or orbiting an Alliance planet; shields up, weapons ready, energy use high.

WHY: The condition decides what works. Phasers only fire in condition RED, and the crew only recovers its rest in GREEN or DOCKED. With AAS on, the ship changes condition by itself.

# Star system number (SS-##)
@shelf BRIDGE
@see Training 2.2 Bridge Display
HOW: 1) Look for SS- and a number at the top left, next to EDC. 2) That is the star system you are in; a region has 50, numbered 0 to 49. 3) SS-XX means you are in hyperspace, between systems. 4) Planets are named after it: SS-3A is planet A of system 3.

WHY: Orders, probe reports and messages all name places by this number, so it is the quickest answer to "where am I?".

# Orbital display
@shelf BRIDGE
@see Training 2.2 Bridge Display
@see Ops 1.1 Navigation Control
HOW: 1) The planet is in the centre; three rings of dots are the high, medium and low orbits. 2) Your ship and anything else in orbit ride on the rings. 3) Corner letters describe the planet: see the card "Orbital display legend". 4) The planet's name is green if Krellan, red if Alliance, blue if neutral, and UNKNOWN in white until surveyed.

WHY: It replaces the tactical display while you orbit. The lower your orbit, the faster you circle the planet, which is how you catch up with another ship in orbit.

# Heading angles
@shelf BRIDGE
@see Training 2.2 Bridge Display
HOW: 1) Four numbers sit around the tactical display. 2) The top one is your heading: the way the nose points. 3) Right is starboard, bottom is aft, left is port, always 90 degrees apart. 4) Angles run clockwise: 0 is up the map, 90 right, 180 down, 270 left. 5) Circle Pad left or right turns the ship one degree at a time.

WHY: Bearings to targets are given in the same angles. If a ship bears 90, it is on the right-hand side of the system map, whichever way you are facing.

# Hull damage bar
@shelf BRIDGE
@see Training 2.2 Bridge Display
HOW: 1) Find the upright bar labelled Hull in the DAMAGE panel. 2) Green is sound hull, yellow is damage, in steps of 20%. 3) The word Hull is cyan with no damage and orange with any. 4) Over 50% damage lights HULL PN: tap STARFORT and dock.

WHY: Past half damage, every further hit breaks ship systems more easily. The manual's advice at that point: get to the nearest starfort immediately.

# Systems status: the twelve names
@shelf BRIDGE
@see Training 2.2 Bridge Display
HOW: COMPUTR main computer. S/L ENG sublight engines. HYP ENG hyperdrive engines. SRS short range sensors. LRS long range sensors. SHD CTL shield control. TRP CTL torpedo control. PHS CTL phaser control. TELEPRT teleporters. COM CTL communications. TRAC BM tractor beam. PLS primary life support.

WHY: When an order fails, look here for the matching system. No sublight engines means no moving, no HYP ENG means no trips between stars, no TELEPRT means no landing parties.

# Weapons status (P T T)
@shelf BRIDGE
@see Training 2.2 Bridge Display
@see Tutorial 4: Weapons
HOW: 1) P is the phaser bank; the two T are the torpedo tubes. 2) Green: ready to fire. Red: reloading, recharging or on standby. Black: out of action. 3) The small letter is a for automatic aiming or m for manual. 4) A blinking "cont" means continuous fire is on.

WHY: X and Y only do something when the matching light is green. After each shot the light goes red for a short time while the weapon gets ready again.

# Switch lights: ECM, SPD, RST, CMF, AAS
@shelf BRIDGE
@see Training 2.2 Bridge Display
@see Training 2.4 Ship and Simulation Controls
HOW: 1) ECM, magenta: jamming on; enemy weapons cannot lock on you, and yours cannot lock on them. 2) SPD, blue: flight recorder on. 3) RST, white: crew resting. 4) CMF, orange: only essential messages shown. 5) AAS, green: shields and red alert are automatic; magenta "man" means manual. 6) Tap ECM, or SHIP MENUS, CONTROLS then Hot keys, to change a switch.

WHY: These five switches change how the ship behaves without any menu showing it, so check them when the ship acts oddly, for example shields that will not go up by themselves.

# Supply ship, sideslip and tractor lights
@shelf BRIDGE
@see Training 2.2 Bridge Display
HOW: 1) Supply ship symbol above ECM: grey means none is coming; green with a number means one is on its way, that many light years off. 2) ss with a green arrow: the ship is sideslipping, flying 45 degrees off its heading; two arrows mean evasive maneuvers. 3) TB lit magenta: your tractor beam is on.

WHY: A supply ship can refill you without a trip to a starfort. Sideslip and evasive maneuvers make you harder to hit. The tractor beam uses energy and needs your shields down.

# EDC: enemy distress call
@shelf BRIDGE
@see Training 2.2 Bridge Display
HOW: 1) EDC sits at the top left, beside the star system number. 2) Lit red, your communications section has picked up one or more enemy distress calls. 3) For details tap SHIP MENUS, then COMMS, then Status.

WHY: An enemy calling for help is an enemy in trouble somewhere, and it may be bringing other enemy ships to that spot.

# TARGET DATA
@shelf BRIDGE
@see Training 2.2 Bridge Display
@see Tutorial 4: Weapons
HOW: 1) At red alert this replaces DATA; the number in its title is torpedoes left. 2) Each line is one enemy: R.Pos (position from you, in points), Brng (bearing), Vel (speed) and its symbol. 3) Wpn shows which weapon is locked on it: P or T, red for destroy, yellow for disable, blue for standby; a white background means manual aim.

WHY: It tells you whether a target is in reach (phasers 5 points, torpedoes 10) and which weapon will hit what before you press X or Y.

IF IT DOESN'T WORK: To see your stores during a fight, tap SHIP MENUS, then CONTROLS, then Hot keys, and switch the Data/Target display.

# Can't fire
@shelf PROBLEMS
@see Ops 1.3 Combat Control
@see Tutorial 4: Weapons
HOW: Check in this order. 1) STATUS must be RED for phasers. 2) The P or T light must be green; red means wait. 3) Is the target close enough? Phasers 5 points, torpedoes 10. 4) Is ECM lit? Switch it off. 5) Tap SHIP MENUS, COMBAT, Weapons: a weapon on Standby never fires.

IF IT DOESN'T WORK: From open space you cannot hit a ship that is in orbit: go into orbit too. In orbit, phasers cannot shoot through the planet (the P blinks): use torpedoes or change your orbit.

# Can't enter hyperspace
@shelf PROBLEMS
@see Ops 1.1 Navigation Control
@see Tutorial 1: Basic Navigation
HOW: 1) Hyperspace trips must be set with the Targeter on the STARS or WAR map: you cannot steer into one. 2) The first target must be another star system. 3) The speed set must be 1.0 or more. 4) Tap ENGINES and let the ship reach the system edge at 0.6 or faster. 5) Check HYP ENG in DAMAGE.

# Speed, distance and time units
@shelf REFERENCE
@see Training 2.1 The Galactic Region
@see Ops 1.1 Navigation Control
HOW: What the numbers mean.

Light years: distance on the STARS and WAR maps. A region is 35 wide and 15 deep.

Points: distance inside a star system, which is 80 by 80. The tactical display shows 11 by 11, the LRS 40 by 40.

c-factor: speed, where 1.0 is the speed of light. Sublight runs 0.1 to 0.9. Hyperspace runs 1 to 10; 8 is the safe maximum.

Days: all mission time. A trip's length is shown in the Targeter before you commit.

Degrees: 0 is up the map, 90 right, 180 down, 270 left.

Energy units: a standard battlecruiser holds 4000.

WHY: Weapon ranges are in points (phasers 5, torpedoes 10), probe range is in light years (5), and docking or orbiting needs you within 1 point.

# Ranks and your career
@shelf REFERENCE

WHY: Your rank decides which commands work. If a menu item answers that it is not available at your rank, that is normal: it unlocks later. Practice missions never change your career.

# Ranks and what each one commands
@shelf REFERENCE
@see Training 2.0 Your Missions
HOW: Seven ranks, five missions (A to E) each.

1 Altern: your ship only. Exploration and landing parties.

2 Centurion: ship and four escorts. Ship-to-ship combat.

3 Sub-Commander: ship, escorts, invasion. Advanced space combat.

4 Commander: ship, escorts, invasion. Planetary invasions.

5 Prefect: ship, escorts, invasions. Advanced planetary invasions.

6 Sub-Admiral: unrestricted command. Strategic command.

7 Admiral: unrestricted command. Advanced strategic command.

After mission 7-E: Imperial Tribune, free to replay any mission as practice.

WHY: Each level teaches one skill before the next is added, and the number of Alliance starbases in a region grows from one at level 1 to five at level 7.
